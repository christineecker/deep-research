## What this covers

Lesson 1 (`lessons/0001-what-is-deep-research.html`) established the entry-point mental model:

- **Scope check**: deep-research is for multi-paper synthesis, not single-paper summaries,
  meta-analysis, or paywall bypass.
- **Profile shorthand**: `fast` (10, no gates) / `standard` (25, default) / `systematic` (60,
  dual screening + PRISMA) / `max` (100, + grey lit/web).
- **Target shorthand**: wiki mode (default, needs wiki-manager) vs. standalone repo mode (no
  wiki, right for manuscript-only deliverables).
