Critical appraisal

Appraisal is design-specific and span-backed.

The tool selects a framework based on study design and records domain judgements with source support.

Framework Routing

DesignFrameworkImportant boundary
Randomized trialRoB2Result-specific risk of bias.
Non-randomized intervention studyROBINS-IEffect-of-interest must be named.
Cohort or case-controlNewcastle-Ottawa ScaleReport star count, not a generic quality label.
Systematic reviewAMSTAR-2Review appraisal, not primary-study appraisal.
Diagnostic accuracyQUADAS-2Per index test/reference standard; separates applicability.
Prediction modelPROBASTPer model and development/validation target.
Qualitative studyCASP qualitativeReport checklist count, not risk of bias.
Prevalence estimateJBI prevalenceReport checklist count.
Cross-sectional associationJBI analytical cross-sectionalReport checklist count.
No suitable tool or abstract-onlytool: "none"Record why appraisal was not performed.

Single-Paper Summaries

For one-paper summaries, appraisal is part of the default path. If full text is missing, the design is unsupported, or the user explicitly passes --no-appraise, the summary must state why appraisal was skipped.

Project Scope

Appraisals live under data/papers/appraisals/<project>/. The same paper may have different appraisal targets or certainty implications in different manuscript projects.

Source manuals: references/appraisal.md and references/schema/08-appraisal.md.
