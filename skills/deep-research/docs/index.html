PubMed-centred research workflow

deep-research turns scientific questions and papers into verified, reusable evidence artifacts.

Use it for full literature reviews, manuscript-oriented evidence projects, and lightweight one-paper or selected-paper summaries — all backed by immutable source snapshots, character-offset extraction spans, and appraisal records that a script re-checks rather than a reader trusting.

A reusable extracted-paper pool, no reportA run with pipeline.stop_after_stage: 5Extractions every later run reuses instead of redoing
Manuscript project without wiki-manager/deep-research:init and /deep-research:project createStandalone project, reusable paper pool, exports
Boundary: selected-paper summaries are not systematic reviews. They may orient the reader across a bounded set, but they do not claim comprehensive retrieval, pooled effects, or body-of-evidence certainty — and the verifier rejects that wording outside a run that actually produced it.

Work done once is available everywhere. A paper extracted by a review is reused by a later one-paper summary; a paper registered for a summary is already in the pool the next review seeds from. Appraisals are the deliberate exception — they are scoped to the project whose question they answer.

The Core Rule

Scientific claims flow through structured records: source snapshot → extraction span → appraisal domain → summary record → verification. The tool does not free-form summarize from a PDF when a structured extraction should exist, and it does not let an agent supply its own quotation. Every excerpt in a finished artifact was cut from an immutable snapshot by a script — once at assembly, once at publish.

Invariants That Never Bend

#Rule
1No PubMed-backed claim without a PMID actually retrieved this run.
2No claim from any source without a retrieved evidence id: PMID, DOI, PMCID, preprint DOI, guideline URL, report URL, or bundle-relative concept path.
3Abstract-only vs. full-text is tagged on every extraction. Abstract-only is never appraised as if full text.
4Null and negative findings are actively searched for. Screeners must not exclude on outcome positivity.
5Conflicts are surfaced with an explanation, never averaged away.
6"New insights / hypotheses" is hard-walled from "what the evidence shows".
7Retracted and Expression-of-Concern papers are flagged at screening and at every point of use.
8PubMed attribution is honored: cite PubMed and DOIs.
9No paywall circumvention of any kind. No credentials, sessions, proxies, captcha solving, or mirrors; no retrying a 401/402/403 by another route. Fail closed.
10-11Bundle concepts preserve full PubMed metadata; per-claim attribution uses footnotes keyed to source ids. A body-only citation list is not acceptable.
12-13The wiki's own wiki/ tree is never written to; standard markdown links are the graph layer, wikilinks additive only.
14Unknown values are null. Never invent a bibliographic field, a number, or a citation.
15Whenever a PDF is handed to you, its DOI is stated alongside the PMID — or the absence of a DOI is stated.
