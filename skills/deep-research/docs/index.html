Boundary: selected-paper summaries are not systematic reviews. They may orient the reader across a bounded set, but they do not claim comprehensive retrieval, pooled effects, or body-of-evidence certainty.

Core Evidence Rule

Scientific claims should flow through structured records: source snapshots, extraction spans, appraisal domains, summary records, then verification. The tool avoids direct free-form summarization from PDFs when a structured extraction should exist.
